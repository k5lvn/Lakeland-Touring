OWNER’S GARAGE · NEW BOOKING PLAN

£49 a year. £25 per booking.

Review version: owner-admin-20261021. This new plan applies only to vehicles whose owners explicitly accept it. Existing advertising agreements keep their recorded terms until acceptance. The new booking service requires activation by Touring.

Clear, affordable fees

The annual advertising fee is £49 per vehicle for 12 months from activation. There is no automatic renewal. Accepting this plan does not extend an existing annual term or take payment.

A flat £25 admin fee helps cover each confirmed booking. Enquiries and temporary date holds do not incur that fee. The fee comes from the owner’s £250 booking deposit, leaving £225 of that deposit with the owner. It becomes due when the full balance is confirmed paid or 12 weeks before arrival, whichever comes first.

Bookings and customer payments

Touring records and confirms the booking. The independent vehicle owner receives the customer’s hire payments and confirms actual receipt of the £250 deposit and full balance in Owner’s Garage. These confirmations do not move money or verify a bank account. The £25 admin fee is paid to Touring by the owner; it is not added to the customer’s hire price.

Customer contact details stay hidden from the owner until the owner confirms receipt of the full balance. Contact Touring using the booking reference before that point. Keep bookings arranged through Touring on the platform. Diverting them off the platform may end advertising rights following review.

Collection checks

Collection is by appointment, Monday to Friday between 09:00 and 16:00, excluding notified closure dates. Identity, driving licence and insurance evidence must be checked at least two working days before collection. A confirmed booking and full balance are also required before handover.

Cancellation and responsibilities

The agreed booking policy is that cancellation within 12 weeks of arrival loses the booking deposit. A £25 admin fee already earned remains due. Recording cancellation does not issue a refund; Touring must review the booking’s applicable cancellation and refund terms. The £250 booking deposit is separate from any damage or security deposit.

The owner remains responsible for the vehicle, accurate advertising, lawful hire, maintenance, insurance and their hire agreement. Final hire, insurance, damage-deposit and refund arrangements must be supplied before the service launches. This review page must be checked with those documents before activation.
