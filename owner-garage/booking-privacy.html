OWNER’S GARAGE

Booking privacy notice

Review version for the new owner booking service. Lakeland Touring, part of Worx365 Group, records bookings, customer names and contact details, hire amounts, dates, the accepted pricing version, payment confirmations, appointments and evidence-check references.

Access to booking information

Touring administrators can see booking contact details. Each vehicle owner can see only their own bookings. Customer names, email addresses and telephone numbers are withheld from the owner until they confirm receipt of the full balance. Booking records and confirmation history are not public.

Confirmation records

We record the account, action and time of payment confirmations, booking confirmations, evidence checks and handover. Payment and evidence references support review; the garage does not verify bank accounts or store copies of identity, licence or insurance documents. Keep those documents in an agreed secure location and enter only the reference here.

Contact LakelandTouring@Worx365Group.uk about your information. The existing Garage privacy notice covers your account. Retention periods, secure evidence storage and the complete booking privacy information must be finalised before activation.
