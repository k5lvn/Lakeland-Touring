# Touring operational review — 21 October 2026

The booking candidate remains disabled and unpublished. This is separate from the already-live LD networking release and Performance 365 activity refresh.

Review found that verified identity/licence/insurance checks could not be withdrawn. A mistaken or withdrawn policy could therefore remain marked valid. The new `invalidate-evidence` action allows only the booking owner or Touring administrator to withdraw an existing check, with CSRF protection, explicit confirmation and a reason. It clears the active verification and appends actor/time/reason plus the previous verification time, actor and reference to the immutable audit table. Repeat withdrawal does not duplicate history. Rechecking uses a new server timestamp and the ordinary two-working-day requirement. Cancelled or handed-over bookings remain closed to changes.

Local validation: 62 real HTTP permission/CSRF/isolation/withdrawal/replacement/audit checks and 22 fee/cancellation/handover policy checks passed. CI exercises owner withdrawal/replacement on phone and desktop, plus all existing booking suites. Its final result must be checked before release.

Confirmed reviewed policy: £49 per vehicle for 12 months plus £25 per confirmed booking; new-plan acceptance required, no silent migration. Owner confirms received customer money; Touring confirms bookings and its fee receipt. Fee comes from the owner's £250 booking deposit at the earlier full-balance/12-week point. No bank transfer, refund or customer confirmation email is sent by these controls. Stripe checkout and the owner ledger remain separate.

Launch facts/processes still required:

- Own-Elddis flow: actual hire periods/rates, booking horizon and blocked dates; Stripe merchant/webhook setup and verified private reconciliation scheduler; actual caravan plate/year/dimensions/weight, hire insurance and refundable damage/security-deposit rules.
- Owner marketplace: final hire/cancellation/refund/insurance documents consistent with the new terms; actual annual-term transition and owner acceptance; closure dates; secure evidence storage/retention/correction/dispute process; admin login/MFA and deployment/staging check against existing listings.
- Campsite guides: operator acceptance of externally hired caravans and pitch/access suitability for the actual caravan. Published campsite descriptions alone do not establish this.

No unknown dates, rates, insurer permissions or vehicle specifications have been invented. Do not activate bookings or overwrite existing owner contracts merely because CI passes.
