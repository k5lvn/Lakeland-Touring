TWO WAYS TO HIRE

Choose your escape.
We’ll make it easy.

OPTION ONE

Self-tow

Collect the caravan in Carlisle, tow it to your chosen destination and return it at the end of your hire. Suitable insurance and towing arrangements must be confirmed with us before booking.

Ask about self-tow ↗

OPTION TWO

Delivery, setup & collection

We deliver the caravan to your approved campsite, set it up for your stay, then return to dismantle and collect it.

Delivery charge: £1.50 per mile, calculated across all four journeys: delivery out and back, then collection out and back. Setup, dismantling and collection are included; there is no separate setup fee.

Example: a campsite 100 miles away is 400 chargeable miles in total, so delivery costs £600.

OPTIONAL EXTRA

DJI portable power station

Add a DJI power station to your hire for £10 per day or £69 per week. Ask us to include it in your quotation.

Campsite pitch fees are separate and paid directly to your chosen campsite. Availability, access, delivery distance and campsite approval must be confirmed before a booking is agreed. Your quotation will confirm the hire arrangement, inclusions, payment terms and any deposit.
