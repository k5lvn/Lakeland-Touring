How the new plan works

Bookings are arranged and confirmed through Lakeland Touring. The independent owner receives the customer’s hire payments and confirms receipt. The £25 admin fee is an owner charge; it is not added to the customer’s hire price.

The owner remains responsible for the vehicle, accurate listing, hire agreement, maintenance, insurance and handover requirements. The new plan and hire terms must be agreed before a booking is confirmed.
