Caravan hire FAQs.

Practical answers for a six-berth self-tow holiday from Carlisle, from choosing dates to checking your towing setup. Ask us to confirm the arrangements for your own booking before paying.

How do I check dates or book?

Online availability and checkout are currently unavailable. Send your collection and return dates, number of guests and intended destination by WhatsApp, email or phone. We will confirm availability, the hire price and payment arrangements. An enquiry does not reserve dates.

Where do I collect the caravan?

Collection is in Carlisle, Cumbria. Confirm the exact collection point, handover appointment and return time with us before arranging your journey. Allow time for a handover and questions about the caravan.

How many people can stay?

Our Elddis Avanté 866 is advertised as a six-berth caravan. Browse the ten photographs, including the lounge, bunks, rear sleeping area, kitchen and washroom, then ask us about the sleeping arrangements for your party before booking.

Can I hire for a short break?

The published guide rates include short breaks outside peak periods from £395 for three nights. Seven-night guide rates are £595 low season, £650 standard and £795 peak. Your dates, length of stay and any holiday-period rules must be confirmed with us; these figures are not a live quote.

What deposit and balance will I pay?

The published booking arrangement is a £250 booking deposit for bookings more than 12 weeks ahead, with full hire payment for bookings within 12 weeks. Confirm the balance due date and all payment terms before paying. A refundable security deposit is separate; its amount and conditions must be confirmed with your hire arrangements.

Is a campsite pitch included?

No. You book and pay for your campsite separately. Ask the campsite to confirm it accepts a commercially hired, twin-axle caravan and that the allocated pitch and access suit the complete outfit, including any awning. A guide listing does not establish compatibility or availability.

Where can I take the caravan?

Our self-tow hire is advertised for mainland England, Scotland and Wales. Tell us where you plan to travel and agree any restrictions before booking. Use our destination guides for planning ideas; they are not permission to use a particular pitch or route.

What do I need to check before towing?

Before committing, confirm your driving entitlement, tow vehicle limits, towbar and electrical connections, and the actual caravan plated weights and dimensions. Ask us for the caravan details needed for your checks. Do not rely on a generic model listing to decide whether your outfit is suitable.

What is included with the caravan?

Use the gallery to explore the layout and ask us for the equipment and inventory list for your hire. Confirm bedding, towels, gas, electrical hook-up equipment, kitchen items and any awning or extras before packing; photographs alone do not confirm what is included.

Can I bring a dog or arrange delivery?

Ask us before booking about pets, delivery or any other special arrangements. Include your destination and dates so we can confirm what can be offered and any additional cost. Do not assume these services are included.

What happens if I need to cancel?

Ask for the applicable hire agreement, cancellation and refund terms, insurance conditions and security-deposit rules before making payment. These must be agreed for your booking. Contact us promptly if your plans change.

How does the Owner’s Garage plan work?

The new plan is £49 per vehicle per year plus a £25 owner admin fee per confirmed booking. Enquiries and temporary holds do not incur that fee. Existing owners retain their recorded agreement until they accept the new plan. See the Owner’s Garage page for the fee timing and responsibilities.

Send your dates, party size and destination. We can talk through the caravan and your hire arrangements.
